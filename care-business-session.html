BNI Members Event

A relaxed chat about getting into care businesses

Chris has put 45 minutes in Paul's diary for BNI members who are curious about care businesses. It is a chance to ask questions, hear what the work is really like, and have an open conversation with someone who has been doing it for a long time.

If you would like to come, contact Chris or Agota. A couple of bullet points or questions in advance would be useful, but there is no need to prepare anything fancy.

Date Friday 9 October 2026
Time 11:00 am

What we will chat about

This is just a chance to ask Paul what you have been wondering about and get a better feel for the care sector before you take things any further.

  • What running and working in care is actually like.
  • Questions about people, operations, referrals, and service quality.
  • Anything else you are curious about.

If you can, send this ahead

It will help Paul make the conversation useful if you send:

  • A couple of things you would like to understand.
  • Any questions you already have.
  • Names of any other BNI members who might enjoy it.

Who is it for?

Anyone in BNI who is curious about care, whether you are thinking about referrals, partnerships, or simply want to ask a few questions.

Meet Paul

Director of Care at Thrive Homecare

Paul will lead the Q&A. He has 30 years of experience in health and adult social care and oversees the delivery of Thrive's services, including care standards and compliance.
